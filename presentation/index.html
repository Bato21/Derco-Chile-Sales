09 · Model B — predicting loss-making deals

Flag the money-losers before they close.

Supervised classifier on pre-sale features only (brand, channel, comuna, price, timing) — margin excluded to avoid leakage. Baselines → HistGradientBoosting, stratified split + 5-fold CV.

0.83
ROC-AUC — 5-fold CV mean (0.855 on hold-out; stable, std 0.02)

better than random at spotting a loss
50%
of all loss deals caught by reviewing just the top-10% riskiest

Honest note: losses are 2.18% of deals (imbalanced) → the model is a triage aid, a human reviews every flag.

10 · What it all means

Five insights, ranked by decision impact.

  • China won the shelf — 12% → 52% of sales. No longer a hedge; it's the core business.
  • Transactional, not relational — 86% buy once; 14% Champions drive 26% of margin.
  • ~6.8 bn CLP leaks through 2.18% loss-making deals — model catches ~50% for review.
  • Single-brand dependence — Suzuki ≈ 39% of volume.
  • Geographic over-exposure — top-10 comunas ≈ 33% of sales.
▶ BaptisteInsights
11 · The recommendation — one decision

Rebalance the commercial model on three moves.

1 · Retain

Fund a Champions-retention + one-timer-conversion loyalty CRM. Highest-return margin.

2 · Formalise China

Treat Chinese brands as a first-class portfolio — pricing, stock, marketing. It's already the majority.

3 · Plug the leak

Deploy the classifier as a pre-approval check on the riskiest 10% of deals.

▶ BaptisteRecommendation
12 · Ethics, bias & limits

What this analysis cannot say.

  • Synthetic money — all CLP figures are relative signals, not audited financials. Direction trustworthy, level not.
  • Privacy — customer hash is re-identifiable → treated as personal data; address excluded from all models.
  • Geographic bias — Santiago-metro over-represented; "valuable customer" really means "urban customer".
  • Human in the loop — imbalanced target + no causality → models advise, people decide. Re-check yearly.
